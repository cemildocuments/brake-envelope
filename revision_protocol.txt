Review extension protocol, fixed before these extension runs on 2026-11-19.
Purpose: remove exact coefficient observation from calibration, quantify the price of bounded measurement error, and compare against a jointly calibrated functional grid.
Physics and population families remain those in protocol.txt. The main experiment additionally includes Joint-grid: one maximum score across 201 points including the zero-speed limit and V, followed by a single conformal quantile. It should be equivalent to Endpoint; no statistical gain over joint functional calibration is hypothesized.
New independent seeds 2000-2039 per population. Train 4000, scale 2000, calibrate 511, test 10000; alpha=.05, same model hyperparameters and clearance law. One latent episode supplies two noisy distance measurements at u1=.75 and u2=2.5 m/s, with the SAME coefficients across these paired trials. All learning labels are reconstructed from noisy pairs. Calibration labels are never clipped.
Noise bounds eta=0,.01,.025,.05 meters. For positive eta, two profiles: independent symmetric Uniform[-eta,eta], and under-reading Uniform[-eta,0]. The bound used by the robust method is the same absolute eta in either case (bias direction is not supplied). Zero eta is evaluated once. Noise draws for disjoint folds use independent streams. The same latent folds and uniforms are reused across configurations within a seed, so comparisons are paired.
Methods: Oracle-label Endpoint uses latent calibration coefficients but shares the fits trained on noisy labels; Naive Endpoint uses reconstructed coefficients without an error allowance; Robust Endpoint maximizes the score over the rectangular distance-error set and continuous speed interval. This maximum is attained at speed 0 or V and one of the measurement-error rectangle corners; its closed form uses the absolute interpolation weights. All test risks use latent physical coefficients. No hyperparameter or noise bound is selected from test outcomes.
Sensitivity at eta=.025, under-reading profile: u1=.3 and 1.5, u2 fixed at 2.5, alongside primary u1=.75. Retain every setting regardless of outcome.
Summaries: mean across four populations within each seed block, then 95% t intervals across 40 blocks. Paired speed loss relative to Oracle-label; paired risk differences to Naive. Total unique latent test episodes: 1.6 million reused across noise settings; do not count repeats as new episodes.
Numerical verification: measurement inversion; robust closed form versus a dense speed grid and all four error corners; true-score domination; zero-error equality; eight/twelve-worker deterministic metrics. Microbenchmark score computation with n=511, 201 joint-grid points, 1000 repeats after 20 warmups; separately report fit cost. Timings are hardware-dependent and are not end-to-end controller latency.
Extension is a braking-specific implementation of established bounded-label-error conformal protection, not a new general noisy-label theorem. Unbounded or invalid error bounds and within-pair parameter drift are outside its guarantee.
